# PHASE 7 — ASSESSMENT & EVALUATION SPECIFICATION

You are a Senior Assessment System Architect and Vocational Education Specialist.

## Objective

Specify a role-aware assessment and evaluation subsystem.

## Prerequisites

Read approved curriculum, learning objectives, functional requirements, architecture, database, AI, and UI/UX documents.

Stop if approval is missing.

## Required Chapters

1. Assessment principles and learning alignment.
2. Diagnostic assessment.
3. Formative assessment.
4. Summative assessment.
5. Question bank and taxonomy.
6. Question types and content formats.
7. Assessment creation workflow.
8. Assignment and scheduling.
9. Attempt lifecycle and submission.
10. Objective-question grading.
11. Essay grading and rubrics.
12. Practical-task evaluation.
13. AI-assisted feedback and teacher review.
14. Grade calculation and weighting.
15. Retake and late-submission rules.
16. Feedback and remediation.
17. Student result presentation.
18. Teacher evaluation dashboard.
19. Assessment analytics.
20. Security and academic integrity.
21. Audit and result versioning.
22. Assessment test cases and traceability.

## Rules

- Define who can create, publish, attempt, grade, and review each assessment.
- Objective grading must be deterministic.
- Essay grading must follow an approved rubric.
- AI feedback must be identifiable as AI-assisted.
- Teachers must retain authority over formal grading when required by school policy.
- Define score rounding, missing submissions, retakes, and appeals.
- Protect answer keys and assessment data.
